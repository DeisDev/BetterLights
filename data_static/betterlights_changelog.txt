[b]Better Lights v1.6.0[/b]

[list]
[*]Added a dedicated, searchable settings window with an optional key bind, a toggling Sandbox desktop shortcut, and a streamlined Quick Settings entry in the Spawn Menu.
[*]Added settings window appearance and navigation options for opacity, icons, and compact spacing.
[*]Improved settings search, keyboard navigation, page and scroll restoration, resizing, and small-screen layout.
[*]Improved settings pages with clearer enabled, disabled, locked, and server-controlled states, plus quick controls for groups of effects.
[*]Improved the global flashlight settings menu with clearer override states and unapplied-change feedback.
[*]Added server-wide flashlight settings.
[*]Added a personal option to disable Better Lights.
[*]Added CAMI permission support so server owners can delegate access to Better Lights server settings without granting full admin access.
[*]Added a dedicated Profiles category for saving, loading, importing, exporting, and resetting personal settings, with clearer selection, quicker saving, and double-click loading.
[*]Added smaller profile files and compact sharing codes while preserving existing JSON import, viewing, and copying.
[*]Added a muzzle flash blacklist.
[*]Polished the in-game changelog, which now opens after updates by default and includes a Never show again option.
[*]Added a configurable third-person flashlight spill light with adjustable radius and either beam-matched or custom brightness and color.
[*]Added separate flashlight attachment and position controls for first person, third person, and other players.
[*]Added a personal flashlight attachment blacklist, including a shortcut for blacklisting the held weapon.
[*]Fixed player models sometimes blocking their own flashlight beams in third person or when viewed by other players.
[*]Fixed flashlight placement on the Crossbow by preventing it from using weapon attachments.
[*]Fixed flashlight placement when using the Hands weapon in Hide and Seek.
[*]Improved flashlight centering and close-range coverage with default and ARC9 weapons.
[*]Added configurable flashlight lens flares with improved placement and color.
[*]Added advanced flashlight shadow settings with more stable shadows near props.
[*]Improved flashlight compatibility with camera, input, battery HUD, Oxygen, and Stamina addons, and corrected flashlight sounds for nearby players.
[*]Added a configurable global light budget with separate dynamic, entity, and projected light limits, optional distance fading, and view-aware prioritization.
[*]Added optional persistent eye and ambient lights to supported NPC ragdolls.
[*]Added configurable eye lights for player models, including first-person and multiplayer options.
[*]Added optional eye lights for Dog.
[*]Improved Rollermine lights and settings for regular, hacked, and temporary power-down states.
[*]Added separate green lighting controls for hacked Manhacks.
[*]Added flashlight, muzzle flash, projectile, and explosion compatibility for Customizable Weaponry 2.0 and TFA Base weapons.
[*]Added ArcCW flashlight and muzzle flash compatibility.
[*]Improved MW Base muzzle flash handling and added flashlight attachment support for MW Base weapons.
[*]Improved ARC9 flashlight and muzzle flash placement so active attachments, muzzle devices, and suppressors are handled more accurately.
[*]Added M9K flashlight compatibility and integration settings for MW Base, ArcCW, and ARC9.
[*]Added compatibility with the Hexa Core Shield Generator, preventing its energy core from triggering repeated explosion lights.
[*]Added impact lights for confirmed Stun Stick hits.
[*]Added menu controls for Combine Ball lighting targets, RPG explosion flashes, and showing the held Crossbow light only while loaded.
[*]Improved muzzle flash placement and compatibility across supported weapon bases and weapons with real muzzle attachments.
[*]Fixed false or repeated gunfire lights from non-gun weapons, unsupported weapons, some NPC and NextBot attacks, and shotgun pellets.
[*]Fixed muzzle flashes and bullet impact lights appearing for shots that did not actually fire.
[*]Improved explosion flashes so they originate reliably and avoid repeats from damaged targets or removed entities.
[*]Improved Scanner and Hunter Chopper searchlights so they follow the real spotlight state and direction, use model attachments reliably, respond to changed settings, and clean up correctly.
[*]Improved held and dropped weapon light placement and flashlight weapon attachment handling.
[*]Improved Antlion Worker glow placement and Antlion spit impact detection.
[*]Fixed fire lights remaining visible when their size or brightness was set to zero.
[*]Fixed eye glow appearing on some custom Combine NPC models.
[*]Fixed the Combine Mine idle glow toggle.
[/list]
