[b]Better Lights[/b]

[i]Configurable dynamic lighting for Garry's Mod.[/i]

[img]https://files.catbox.moe/8uylmi.gif[/img]

[hr][/hr]

[b]Overview[/b]

Better Lights adds highly configurable dynamic lighting effects to most parts of the game. Some of these things include: 

[list]
[*]A spiffy new flashlight with a whole bunch of settings
[*]Fire, explosions, projectiles, bullet impacts and muzzle flashes can now emit light
[*]NPCs emit light where it makes sense
[*]NPCs like the combine can emit light from their eyes
[*]A bunch more cool stuff for you to check out!
[/list]

[img]https://files.catbox.moe/z9kpr2.gif[/img]

[hr][/hr]

[b]Settings[/b]

Starting with Better Lights v1.6.0, settings are available in a dedicated window that you can open from any gamemode, not just Sandbox.

There are four ways to open the settings window:

[list]
[*]Enter [b]betterlights_menu[/b] in the console.
[*]Assign an optional menu shortcut in Utilities > Better Lights > Quick Settings
[*]Select the Better Lights icon from the context menu. 
[*]Open Utilities > Better Lights > Quick Settings and click Open Better Lights Settings.
[/list]


[img]https://files.catbox.moe/okwsv1.gif[/img]

[hr][/hr]

[b]Engine Limits[/b]

Better Lights works inside the normal Source Engine lighting limits. All lighting effects, addons etc. share this limit. Here is what that can look like:  

[list]
[*]Dynamic lights: 32 world lights and 64 entity lights can be active at once.
[*]Shadowed projected textures: 8 can be active by default across flashlights, map lights, and addon spotlights.
[*]If the limit is reached, lights may disappear, flicker, or be replaced by newer lights.
[/list]


[hr][/hr]

[b]Compatibility[/b]

[list]
[*]Should work with most GShader Library addons.
[*]Includes integrations with most of the popular SWEP bases such as ARC9 and MWBase.
[*]Most features are client-side and can be disabled individually. There are a few server-side effects such as muzzle flashes, flashlight beams and bullet impacts.
[/list]

If you are having trouble with the flashlight, it is very likely you have a mod conflict. Please make sure you check for this before you report an issue.

[hr][/hr]

[b]Recommended[/b]

[b]Use the 64-bit branch of Garry's Mod if you can.[/b]

Better Lights is made and tested on the 64-bit chromium branch, and unless you have a really old pc, I highty recommend you switch over.
